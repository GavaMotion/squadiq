My Team

Your roster, finally organized.

Player profiles with jersey numbers, preferred positions, and per-position star ratings. Run up to four teams from one account.

AI Lineup

No more 6am sub spreadsheets.

Pick a formation, mark who's available, tap once. The planner builds a balanced four-quarter lineup with automatic goalkeeper rotation and "everybody plays" baked in.

¾ Rule & Free Subs

Playing time, handled both ways.

SquadIQ flags anyone short of AYSO's three quarters while you're still building the lineup. And for leagues that sub on the fly, switch to Free Subs: a running clock, every player's real minutes, and the bench sorted least-played first.

Tactics Board

Draw the play, make it stick.

A full sketch board to diagram set pieces, runs, and formations. Drag players, draw arrows, and save your plays for game day.

Practice Planner

Drills with a purpose.

Build practice sessions from a drill library with video demos, timing, and notes — then run them straight from your phone at the field.

Live Standings

The table, always current.

Track wins, points, goals for/against, and goal differential across your division — auto-refreshed so you always know where you stand.

Your data syncs across every device and works offline at the field. Install it like a native app on iOS, Android, Windows, and Mac.
